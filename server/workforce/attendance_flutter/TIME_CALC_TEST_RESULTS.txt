OFFICE TIME CALCULATION TEST RESULTS
====================================

Formula:
Actual Office Time = (Check-out - Check-in) - Network Away Time

No 8-hour cap is applied.

Test 1:
Check-in: 09:00
Check-out: 18:00
Away: 13:00-14:00 (1 hour)
Total span: 9h 0m
Actual office time: 8h 0m
PASS

Test 2:
Check-in: 09:00
Check-out: 20:00
Away: 13:00-14:00 (1 hour)

- Pending locally detected away time is flushed before checkout.
